Who am I in the digital world?

I’m someone who finds happiness in simple things and believes that every day has something worth remembering. This website is a little glimpse into my world, where I share the things I love, and the moments I treasure.♡

the assignment

Same person,
three screens.

This page explores my digital identity on Instagram, Pinterest and TikTok. For each one I ask: Who am I on ______? I look at how I present myself, how I interact with others, how I consume information, and what I contribute. Then I compare the platforms and judge my own habits through media literacy, privacy and digital citizenship.

i.How I present myself

I like keeping my profile simple and pleasing to look at. I mostly share stories rather than posting on my main feed. I show little moments, memories, and things I enjoy, but I also keep some parts of my life private.

ii.How I interact

I mostly follow my friends, classmates, and accounts that interest me. I interact more with people I'm close to through replies, messages, and comments. I'm usually quiet online, but I become more expressive when talking to people I know well.

iii.What I consume

I mostly see posts from friends, funny videos, lifestyle content, and creative inspirations. I enjoy scrolling through stories and discovering new ideas. Before trusting or sharing information, I try to check if it comes from a reliable source.

iv.What I contribute

I usually share stories, repost funny videos, and save posts that catch my interest. I don't post all the time, but when I do, I want to share something meaningful or something that makes me happy. I hope my posts bring a little positivity and show genuine moments of my life.

i.How I present myself

My Pinterest boards show my creative side and the things I find interesting. I save layouts, collages, scrapbook ideas, and different inspirations that match my taste. It's like a little collection of things that represent me without having to show my face.

ii.How I interact

I mostly stay quiet on Pinterest and use it to browse, save, and organize ideas. I don't interact with people as much as I do on other platforms because I see Pinterest more as my personal inspiration space.

iii.What I consume

I usually search for layout ideas, Canva designs, school projects, study tips, outfits, and makeup inspiration. I also look for references for publication materials and other creative projects. I check the pin's source or linked website to see if it leads to reliable information.

iv.What I contribute

I mostly collect and organize ideas rather than upload my own content. I use the things I save as inspiration for my own designs and projects. When I use someone else's work as a reference, I try to give credit to the original creator.
